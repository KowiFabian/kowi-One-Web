# JÚCAR LIVING — KOWI Commercial Pilot

Purpose: use a tourism microbusiness as an end-to-end test of KOWI agents, CRM, finance, marketing, approvals and reporting.

## Offer
KOWI Local Tourism Agent connects content → landing page → agent → lead → booking/cita → CRM → follow-up → revenue attribution.

Normal SaaS test price:
- 129 EUR/month recurring.
- 149 EUR one-time onboarding.
- Variable operating assumption: 12 EUR/client/month.
- Fixed operating assumption: 700 EUR/month.
These are test assumptions, not market-validated prices.

## Customer targets
- 8 paying clients = 1,032 EUR MRR.
- 16 paying clients = 2,064 EUR MRR.
- Month-one normal target: 8 clients; 2,224 EUR revenue including onboarding.

## 12-month scenario summary
Acid: 4 initial clients, 5% monthly growth assumption, 4% churn, 99 EUR ARPA. Year revenue 12,474 EUR; operating contribution 4,209 EUR.
Normal: 8 initial clients, 10% monthly growth assumption, 3% churn, 129 EUR ARPA. Year revenue 24,841 EUR; operating contribution 14,449 EUR.
Optimistic: 12 initial clients, 20% monthly growth assumption, 2% churn, 149 EUR ARPA. Year revenue 81,183 EUR; operating contribution 66,043 EUR.

Operating contribution is before founder salary, personal taxes, financing and property investment.

## Separate tourism-business growth exercise
Baseline Júcar Living revenue assumption: 2,274 EUR/month = 27,288 EUR/year.
- +10% annual: 30,016.80 EUR.
- +20% annual: 32,745.60 EUR.

## Validation gates
Do not buy tourism property merely to validate the software/business model. First validate:
1. lead generation;
2. conversion to booking/sale;
3. repeatable acquisition cost;
4. retention;
5. positive contribution after software and servicing cost;
6. owner workload;
7. data protection and consent flows.
