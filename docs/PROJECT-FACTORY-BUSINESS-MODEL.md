# KOWI PROJECT FACTORY — Business Model

## Purpose
KOWI converts an intention, underused resource or community need into a measurable project. It is not an investment promise or autonomous investment manager.

## Value chain
Intention → opportunity discovery → evidence gathering → feasibility → financial model → risk/impact review → Human Approval → execution plan → operation → evidence → learning → optional reinvestment.

## Customer groups
- Individuals with ideas, property, land or capital.
- SMEs and hospitality/local businesses.
- Project promoters and professional operators.
- Communities and organizations seeking structured local projects.
- Partners/investors who need transparent project reporting.

## Revenue model candidates
1. KOWI Projects subscription.
2. Project setup/feasibility fee.
3. Business Agent recurring subscription.
4. Approved implementation/project-management fee.
5. CRM/operations subscription.
6. Clearly disclosed success fee where legally and contractually appropriate.
Regulated investment intermediation, collective investment or financial advice must not be offered without the required legal analysis/authorisation.

## Project accounting
Every project keeps three ledgers:
- Financial: capital, revenue, cost, cash, debt and return.
- Impact: project-specific human/environmental indicators.
- Partner: contribution, ownership/rights, distributions and attributable return.

## Decision gates
No project advances solely because an optimistic scenario looks attractive. Minimum gates:
1. Need/opportunity evidence.
2. Demand evidence.
3. Legal/urban planning feasibility.
4. Technical feasibility.
5. Funding and downside liquidity.
6. Acid/base/upside scenarios.
7. Privacy/security and environmental requirements.
8. Explicit human approval for commitment.

## Initial verticals
Hospitality upgrade, eco-lodging, housing rehabilitation, rural production, local food, renewable energy, water/resource efficiency, restaurants, coworking and community resilience.

## Agent team
KOWI Orchestrator, Projects Agent, Opportunity & Feasibility Agent, Real Estate & Infrastructure Agent, Community Resilience Agent, Financial Analyst Agent IA PRO, Marketing Agent IA PRO, CRM Agent, Operations Agent, Trust & Security Agent.

## Safety
KOWI never autonomously purchases property, commits funds, signs contracts, takes debt, changes ownership rights or makes regulated investment decisions. Those actions require explicit human approval and, where applicable, qualified external professionals.
