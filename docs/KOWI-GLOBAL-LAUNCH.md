# KOWI Global Launch Campaign v1

## Offer
KOWI Business Basic
- Spain / Western Europe reference: EUR 99 monthly, EUR 990 annual.
- Same product, localized regional price.
- Subscription first. No mandatory setup fee.
- External provider overages are not unlimited and can be billed by usage.

## Positioning
Not: “another chatbot”.
Promise: “KOWI attends customers across connected channels, shows verified business information, captures opportunities and helps reserve/follow up from one simple CRM.”

## Primary launch market
Spain first. Validate one repeatable funnel before broad international paid expansion.

## Core audiences
1. Hair/beauty.
2. Clinics and appointment businesses (administrative use only).
3. Real estate.
4. Workshops/local services.
5. Academies/professionals.
6. Restaurants/hospitality where reservation flow fits.

## Funnel
Ad / organic / partner / referral
→ /business
→ sector demo
→ “Try it with my business”
→ qualified lead
→ configured demo
→ monthly or annual subscription
→ onboarding
→ channel verification
→ activation
→ retention/referral.

## 30-day launch tests
### Test A: Founder + organic
Budget: EUR 0–100.
Goal: 30 qualified business conversations; 10 configured demos; 3 paid customers.

### Test B: ChatGPT Ads Spain
Budget: EUR 300.
Objective: Clicks or conversions when tracking is ready.
OpenAI currently recommends starting CPC max bids around USD 3–5; do not assume this equals actual CPC.
Creative angle: “¿Pierdes clientes porque no puedes contestar WhatsApp, email y reservas?”
Landing: sector-specific KOWI demo.

### Test C: Google Search
Budget: EUR 300.
Intent clusters:
- agente IA WhatsApp negocio
- chatbot WhatsApp empresa
- automatizar reservas WhatsApp
- asistente IA atención clientes
- IA para peluquería / clínica / inmobiliaria

### Test D: Meta
Budget: EUR 300.
Short demo video/static proof-oriented creative.
Retarget site visitors and demo users separately.

### Referral/partner engine
Offer agencies/consultants a channel commission only after customer payment and retention threshold.
Do not mass-message unauthorized lists.

## Initial paid media budget
EUR 900 for the first controlled acquisition cycle:
- ChatGPT Ads 300
- Google 300
- Meta 300
Scale only if qualified CAC and retention support EUR 99 pricing.

## Guardrails
- No fake reviews or customers.
- No fabricated ROI.
- No indiscriminate cold WhatsApp/email spam.
- External channels only after tenant verification.
- Google Business Profile/Maps is not advertised as chat.
- Paid campaigns stop/adjust if CAC or activation economics fail.

## International rollout
Wave 1: Spain.
Wave 2: Portugal, Italy, UK, Mexico, Colombia.
Wave 3: US, Canada, Brazil, India.
Wave 4: broader regional localization after support/compliance/cost validation.

Regional pricing is a testable commercial policy, not a permanent entitlement. Review conversion, margin and churn quarterly.
