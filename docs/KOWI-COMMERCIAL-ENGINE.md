# KOWI Commercial Engine v1

## Goal
Convert KOWI from a broad AI ecosystem into a revenue-producing Human-First AI business without losing the long-term organization/foundation vision.

## Commercial priority
1. KOWI Business: paid AI agent + CRM + follow-up for SMEs/professionals.
2. Implementation/onboarding services: setup, business knowledge, channels and workflows.
3. KOWI School: free entry layer + paid professional/business learning.
4. KOWI Pro: advanced multi-agent, analytics, security and operations.
5. Partnerships: technology, associations, consultancies and education channels.

## Operating model
Revenue & Growth Manager
→ Marketing Agent
→ Sales Executive Agent
→ CRM Agent
→ Business Agent
→ Customer Success Agent
→ Partnerships Agent
→ Finance Agent
→ Insights Agent
→ Security Agent

## Human control
Pricing changes, discounts, contracts, ad spend, public commitments and external messages remain approval-gated.

## Initial funnel
Traffic/community → free diagnostic/demo → qualified lead → discovery → configured demo → proposal → pilot → paid plan → onboarding → adoption → renewal/expansion.

## Early offers
### KOWI Business Launch
AI customer-service/sales agent, CRM, lead follow-up, basic insights.

### KOWI Business Pro
Adds channels, automations, advanced intelligence and operational controls.

### KOWI Setup
One-time implementation/onboarding service.

### KOWI School Professional
Skills path, labs, portfolio and evidence-based credential.

### KOWI School Business
AI literacy and practical upskilling for teams.

## Metrics
Qualified leads, demo-to-proposal, proposal-to-paid, MRR, implementation revenue, activation, retention, expansion, AI cost, contribution margin, time-to-value.

## Rule
No agent can invent customer commitments, prices, case studies, revenue, partnerships or results. Observed facts and projections must be separated.
