# KOWI Campaign Budget Scenarios v1

These are planning assumptions, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Channel benchmark assumptions
For early planning use conservative B2B ranges:
- Meta Ads: €15–€45 per lead.
- Google Search: €30–€70 per lead.
- LinkedIn: €80–€150 per lead.
Local Spanish service lead-gen can be cheaper, but KOWI should not budget as if those lower figures were guaranteed.

## Scenario A — Founder-led validation
Paid media: €0–€150/month.
Focus: founder outreach, referrals, local business visits, LinkedIn organic, short demos, KOWI School content.
Success test: 20 discovery conversations, 5 configured demos, 1–3 paying pilots.
Use before scaling ads.

## Scenario B — Controlled launch
Media: €300/month.
Suggested mix: Meta €200 + Google €100.
Benchmark-derived lead range: approximately 6–17 raw leads before qualification.
Primary goal: learn which message/sector converts, not scale.

## Scenario C — Madrid growth test
Media: €900/month.
Suggested mix: Meta €450 + Google €300 + LinkedIn €150.
Benchmark-derived raw lead range: approximately 15–42.
Primary goal: compare local-service ICPs, demo conversion and cost per qualified opportunity.

## Scenario D — Repeatable acquisition
Media: €2,500/month.
Suggested mix: Meta €1,000 + Google €900 + LinkedIn €400 + creative/retargeting reserve €200.
Benchmark-derived paid lead range: approximately 38–102, before qualification and sales conversion.
Only activate after a repeatable demo-to-paid funnel exists.

## Sales economics guardrail
Do not optimize for cheapest CPL. Track:
lead → qualified → demo → proposal → paid → activated → retained.
Stop or change a campaign when customer acquisition cost and contribution margin do not support the offer.

## Recommended starting rule
Start with Scenario A, then B. Do not jump to D before KOWI has verified:
- offer
- pricing
- demo
- onboarding
- first paid references
- CRM attribution
- contribution margin
